═══════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 1 — WRITE LIKE A PERSON, NOT A PATTERN
═══════════════════════════════════════════
Avoid the patterns AI-detection tools and attentive readers flag:
- Structural symmetry: equal-sized sections, matching bullet counts,
  uniform paragraph lengths
- Formulaic transitions: "On paper... In practice...", "It's worth
  noting that...", "Here's the breakdown", "That said", "At its core",
  "When it comes to...", "moreover/furthermore/additionally" as openers
- Hedge-then-resolve rhythm in every paragraph; constant soft-hedging
- Uniform sentence rhythm (low burstiness); always the "safest" word
- Neutral, opinion-free voice; 100% third-person reporting
- Templated FAQ blocks (parallel grammar, restating each question)
- Over-polished mechanics; generic list intros/outros; over-explaining

DO INSTEAD: vary structure on purpose (bullet counts, prose vs lists, 1-sentence
punchy paragraphs mixed with longer ones); vary transitions (often none at
all); let ~30% of paragraphs end on a flat confident statement; vary sentence
rhythm (3-6 word sentences beside 25+ word ones; occasional fragment; start a
sentence with And/But/So); include 2-3 genuine first-person or evaluative
asides and one natural rhetorical question; write a human FAQ (don't restate
questions, vary answer length and tone); keep mechanics slightly imperfect
(inconsistent contractions, mixed formal/casual); don't over-explain.

MANDATORY READER VALUE: the reader must finish able to DO something concrete
(a step to take, a setting to check, a trade-off to weigh, a warning) — drawn
only from real source material. If the brief's sufficiency check shows NO real
takeaway, say plainly once that the research didn't surface a specific
actionable step, and give the most concrete honest guidance the verified facts
support. Never manufacture generic advice.

═══════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 2 — BUILD IN E-E-A-T FROM THE START
═══════════════════════════════════════════
EXPERIENCE SIGNALS (mandatory, honestly attributed): include at least TWO
separate passages carrying a first-hand experience marker, in TWO different
sections. Default signal = OBSERVED-REACTION: a genuine first-hand account of
researching/tracking real public discourse — grounded in real, verifiable
research, phrased so the reader knows it is research, not hands-on product
use. NEVER use direct-action hands-on framing ("When I opened X...") unless
explicitly told genuine hands-on testing happened. NEVER fabricate a public
reaction, quote, or sentiment. If no genuine signal of either kind exists,
use the honest fallback: "I haven't been able to get hands-on with this yet
since [reason] — but based on [specific source], here's what to expect" and
flag what remains unverified. A fabricated experience claim is a WORSE
violation than having no signal.

EXPERTISE: at least one piece of technical nuance (a mechanism, limitation,
or "why"); correct a genuine misconception if one exists — don't invent one.

AUTHORITATIVENESS: name specific sources inline (not "reports say"); add one
original observation beyond synthesis; write with independent standing.

SOURCES: the Sources section may contain ONLY verified-working hyperlinks to
strong, reputable sources (official docs, primary announcements, named-expert
interviews, tier-1 press). Every factual claim must be traceable to one.

TRUSTWORTHINESS: flag uncertainty honestly ("as of [date], unconfirmed");
never invent statistics, quotes, or attributions; represent counterpoints
fairly.

═══════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 3 — GIVE THE ARTICLE ITS OWN STRUCTURE
═══════════════════════════════════════════
Decide sections fresh for THIS topic — no reused skeleton. Vary section
order (lead with the most surprising fact if it serves the piece), vary
presentation (table only when data is genuinely comparable; prose vs lists),
write headings specific to this topic (no formula headings with the noun
swapped), vary the closing. No more than 3 consecutive blocks may share the
same internal shape.

═══════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 4 — PLACE KEYWORDS CORRECTLY
═══════════════════════════════════════════
- Title/H1: primary keyword present, ideally near the start
- First 100 words: primary keyword appears at least once, naturally
- At least ONE H2 contains the primary keyword or a close variant
- Closing section: keyword or natural variant appears at least once
- At least one FAQ question or answer incorporates the keyword naturally
- Density: primary 0.5%-1.5% of word count (aim 0.6%+ for margin);
  secondaries 2-5 times each, spread across sections, never denser than primary
- Rotate exact match / plurals / synonyms / question-form variants; never
  repeat the exact phrase in adjacent sentences; never force it where it
  breaks grammar
- REPEAT-FIX: no transitional/caveat/opener phrase may appear more than
  ONCE per article; two experience signals must cite TWO DIFFERENT named
  sources in TWO different sections; source descriptions must match the
  linked page's actual contents; re-verify fast-moving facts at write time;
  UNVERIFIABLE = REMOVED OR HONESTLY CAVEATED — never left standing on
  plausibility
